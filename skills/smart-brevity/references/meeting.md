# Smart Brevity — Meeting agenda / notes rules

> Layered on top of `general.md`. Apply both.
>
> Source: *Smart Brevity*, Ch. 16

---

## When this is the right format

- Synchronous time block with multiple people
- Pre-meeting agenda OR post-meeting notes
- 90% admit to daydreaming in meetings; 72% do other work. Plan for skim.

---

## Pre-meeting agenda structure

```
Subject: <6-word tease — meeting topic + action verb>

**Objective:** <One direct sentence — what we'll decide or align on>

**Why it matters:** <1–2 sentences — why THIS group, why NOW>

## Agenda (≤3 items)

1. <Item — bold the decision needed>
2. <Item>
3. <Item>

## Decisions needed

- <Specific decision 1>
- <Specific decision 2>

## Pre-read

<Optional 1-page narrative or 1 link — what to scan in advance>
```

---

## Agenda-specific rules

- **3 agenda items maximum.** If you have 5, pick 3 and defer the rest.
- **Objective is one sentence.** If you can't write it in one sentence, the meeting isn't ready.
- **Decisions needed are specific** — not "discuss roadmap" but "approve Q3 ship date."
- **Pre-read is brief** — 1 page max, or 1 link. The Bezos rule: silent reading at the start of a meeting cures most ills, but the read needs to be readable.

---

## Post-meeting notes structure

```
Subject: <6-word tease — same topic + "recap" or "decisions">

**Decision:** <One sentence — what was decided>

**Why it matters:** <1–2 sentences — what changes because of this>

## What was decided

- <bullet — decision + rationale>
- <bullet>

## Action items

- **<Owner>** — <action> by <date>
- **<Owner>** — <action> by <date>

## Open questions

- <Anything unresolved that needs follow-up>
```

---

## Notes-specific rules

- **Send before close of business** — same day. Memory decays fast.
- **Bullet format only.** No paragraphs.
- **Bold the owner** of each action item — eye-trap so people see their own commitments.
- **Date everything.** "By Friday" is weaker than "by 2026-05-09."
- **Open questions get their own section.** Don't bury them.

---

## During-meeting rules (for facilitators)

- **20 minutes** is usually enough. Slack uses 25 or 50 to leave gap between back-to-backs.
- **Open with the headline** (the one-sentence objective from the agenda).
- **State decisions needed** unambiguously, up front.
- **With 2 minutes left**, summarize takeaways and next steps. Don't run over to do this.
- **Send the follow-up email** before close of business.

---

## Length targets

| Element | Limit |
|---|---|
| Agenda objective | 1 sentence |
| Agenda items | ≤3 |
| Pre-read | 1 page or 1 link |
| Notes total | < 300 words |
| Each action item | 1 line — owner, action, date |
| Meeting length | 20–50 minutes |

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Vague objective ("discuss strategy") | Specific decision: "approve Q3 OKRs" |
| Too many agenda items | Cap at 3; defer the rest |
| No owner on action items | Every action: owner + date, both bold/explicit |
| Notes sent days later | Same-day send; memory decays |
| Recap reads like prose | Bullets only |
| Open questions buried in narrative | Own section; don't hide |
| Chit-chat eats first 5 min | Open on time with the headline |
