# Smart Brevity — Social media rules

> Layered on top of `general.md`. Apply both.
>
> Source: *Smart Brevity*, Ch. 19

---

## When this is the right format

- Short-form post on X/Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads, etc.
- Hand-to-hand combat for attention against an algorithmic feed
- The reader gives you a blink to grab them; if you don't, they're gone

---

## The cardinal principle

**Give, don't ask.**

Algorithms reward giving. Posts that hand the audience an idea, a fact, a laugh, a score, or a moment of surprise outperform posts that beg for clicks, follows, or engagement.

---

## Universal social rules

- **ONE thing per post.** Social forces ruthless selectivity.
- **Strong words, active verbs, no jargon.** Same general rules, harder.
- **Image first.** Words alone underperform on every platform.
- **Concrete over abstract.** "Q3 revenue up 18%" beats "we had a great quarter."
- **Front-load the punch.** First 6 words decide.

---

## Per-platform rules

### Twitter / X

What it rewards: **facts, data, hot quotes, news breaks.** More urgent, better.

- Tease with a newsy nugget; provoke with a surprising quote; wow with a memorable data point
- Emoji to flag tone or signal type (🚨 breaking, 📊 data, 🔥 hot)
- Threads only if the second tweet is as strong as the first
- Skip "thread 🧵" announcements — let the content earn the read

**Good shape (book example):** `🚨 Moon's wet.` — beats a paragraph explaining lunar water discovery.

### Instagram

What it rewards: **eye-catching image + slim, muscular text.**

- The image does ~80% of the work
- Caption is short, specific, scannable
- Often can't link out — the post must stand alone
- Hashtags are signal, not decoration; 3–5 max

### Facebook

What it rewards: **provocative spin on an idea or announcement.**

- Hot take or interesting angle wins
- Snore = vanishes under News Feed waves
- Quote-style framing works ("here's what nobody else is saying about X")

### LinkedIn

What it rewards: **work-relevant insight or professional story with a takeaway.**

- First 2 lines are the hook (the rest collapses behind "see more")
- Lists / numbered takeaways perform well
- Avoid "humblebrag" framing; trust your readers

---

## Length targets

| Platform | Limit / target |
|---|---|
| X/Twitter (single) | 280 chars; aim for ≤200 |
| Instagram caption | ≤150 words; first line is the hook |
| Facebook post | ≤100 words; provocative angle |
| LinkedIn post | ≤200 words; first 2 lines pre-fold |

---

## Emoji rules (Ch. 11)

- **Sparingly.** Once per post is plenty.
- **As tone signal**, not decoration.
- **As shorthand label** for content type:

| Emoji | Signals |
|---|---|
| 🚨 | breaking / alert |
| 📊 | data / chart / poll |
| 🔥 | hot / trending / important |
| ⏰ | deadline |
| 📈 | rising / up / success |
| 📉 | falling / down / decline |
| ✅ | done / 100% |
| 💣 | major news / explosion |
| ⚡ | urgent update |
| 🎯 | precise / targeted |
| 🎙️ | podcast / audio |
| 📺 | TV / video |

Don't pile them up. One signal per post.

---

## What to cut from typical social drafts

- "I'm excited to announce…" — start with what you're announcing
- "Stoked to share…" — same
- "Quick thread on…" — let the content earn the read
- "What do you think? 👇" — desperate; cut
- "Like and share if you agree" — algorithm-bait; cut
- Hashtag stacks (#hustle #grind #leadership #monday) — 3–5 max, signal-shaped

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Burying the news | First 6 words = the news |
| Tweeting paragraphs | One thought per post; thread only if each tweet stands alone |
| Asking for engagement | Give a fact, idea, or laugh; algorithms reward giving |
| Image afterthought | Image first; text supports |
| Emoji spray | One emoji, signaling tone or type |
| Hashtag spam | 3–5 max, signal-shaped, not decorative |
| Generic announcement framing | Lead with the specific, surprising thing |
