# Smart Brevity — Newsletter rules

> Layered on top of `general.md`. Apply both.
>
> Source: *Smart Brevity*, Ch. 13

---

## When this is the right format

- Recurring delivery (daily, weekly) of multiple essential items to a defined audience
- Reader expects to skim ranked items in 5 minutes or less
- Examples: Axios AM, Politico Playbook, internal team digests

---

## Structure

```
<Newsletter name — 1-2 punchy words>

*<word count>, <minutes> minutes*

## 1 big thing: <6-word tease for the lead item>

<One sentence opening the lead item.>

**Why it matters:** <1-2 sentences>

- <bullet>
- <bullet>

[go deeper](link)

---

## 2. <Second item — short headline>

<Lede sentence.>

**Why it matters:** <1-2 sentences>
...

---

## 3. <Third item>
...

---

## 1 fun thing

<Light, personal, or human-interest item to close.>
```

---

## Newsletter-specific rules

### Name (1-2 words)
Must capture the purpose and spirit. Memorable. Brandable.

Examples: Axios AM, 5 Big Things, The Rundown, Click Clack, The Funnel, The TopLine.

### Reading-time marker at top
Always show: `*<N> words, <M> minutes*` — sets the contract with the reader before they invest.

### Lead item: "1 big thing"
- Labeled exactly that — `## 1 big thing:` then the headline (≤6 words)
- Most important item, period
- Signals editorial judgment — "of everything in your inbox today, *this*"

### Numbered subsequent items
- "2.", "3.", "4."… in order of importance
- Tells readers what's coming and how much to budget

### Each item is its own mini-Smart-Brevity
- Headline (≤6 words)
- Lede sentence
- **Why it matters:** (or named axiom) — 1–2 sentences, bolded
- Bullets for any 3+ data points
- Optional `[go deeper](link)`

### Closing item
- "1 fun thing" or "1 smile to go"
- Light, personal, or human — not business
- Ends the newsletter on a relational note

---

## Length targets

| Element | Limit |
|---|---|
| Total items | 5–10 (more = book, not newsletter) |
| Each item | ≤200 words (drop-off is steep past this) |
| Whole newsletter | <1,000 words ideal; 1,200 max |
| Each headline | ≤6 words |
| Each axiom explanation | 1–2 sentences |

---

## Visual rules

- Each item gets one **relevant** image — not decorative, not generic
- Don't slap stock art (Montana frontier on a "we're selling the company" item is the book's own bad-example)
- Charts > clumps of numbers
- Consistent font, text size, layout — readers train themselves into your rhythm

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Too many items (12, 15…) | Cut to top 5–10. The cut is editorial judgment — that's the brand. |
| Items too long (300+ words) | Hard cap at 200; if more is needed, link out via "go deeper" |
| No clear lead item | Always start with "1 big thing" |
| All-business, no human close | End with "1 fun thing" |
| Missing word/time count | Show it at the top |
| Generic images | Image must reinforce the specific item |

---

## Cadence and discipline

- **Same day, same time, every time.** Readers train into the habit.
- Morning send maximizes open rates for daily formats.
- Sunday evening for weekly leadership/exec newsletters.
- Skip a week and the habit breaks. Don't skip.
