## Why
With tariffs and macro headwinds flagged as forward-looking risks, falling prices could help Hawaii margins but simultaneously signal demand risk across the system — the reversal is a double-edged signal.

## Content

### Narrative
Hawaii's refinery sells via contracts indexed to prior-month and prior-week average pricing. In Q1 2026, rapidly rising refined product prices caused realized margin to lag current-period market conditions by ~$15.52/bbl, costing $125.5M vs. what spot-priced sales would have captured. Management noted this lag will reverse if product prices decline. Given that Par Pacific's forward-looking statements separately flag tariff risk and macro uncertainty as headwinds, a price decline could improve Hawaii's reported margin while signaling weaker demand across the broader system.
